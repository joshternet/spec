# Contributing to the Joshternet Specifications

Joshternet specifications are developed publicly through issues, discussion, and pull requests.

Before proposing a change, consider opening an issue when the change affects protocol semantics, interoperability, compatibility, identity, participation, discovery, security, or governance.

Contributions should favor:

- transparency;
- simplicity;
- interoperability;
- voluntary participation;
- open web standards;
- clear separation between identity, participation, discovery, security, and trust.

## Specification status

The Joshternet is currently PRE-JOSH.

All current RFCs are Drafts. Contributions may therefore expose assumptions, change semantics, or identify areas that need further specification.

Future RFC numbers are not reserved in advance. New RFCs should be proposed because a distinct specification is needed, not merely to assign a number.

## Licensing contributions

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise before a contribution is accepted, contributions to this repository are submitted under the same Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License that applies to the repository's specifications and documentation.

Submitting a contribution does not transfer ownership of independently operated websites, Josh identity, implementations, or infrastructure to the Joshternet.

Josh identity is not required to contribute.
